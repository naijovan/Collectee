0:55–2:05 Import
the hero flow		 Tap the plus. Upload two files from demo/ — Charm — Dog Tag and Sovereign Ghost. Start Scan. Watch the step rail fill. On Review: one item is added for you, the other needs a Confirm — tap it. Then Verify → Complete. This is the part that matters. I upload screenshots — no account linking, no API. A vision model reads them and matches each skin against our catalogue, not by name but by what it looks like. High confidence gets added for me. Anything it isn't sure about waits for me to confirm — it never guesses on my behalf. Then I verify, and the items land in my inventory.
2:05–2:25 Inventory & trust From Complete, go to Your Inventory. Point at a Verified badge, then an Unverified one. Scroll one screen. Everything scanned lands unverified, and stays that way until a game account backs it. That distinction is the whole trust model — and it's what decides where an item is allowed to go.
2:25–2:45 Collections Collections tab. Open Crown Jewels. Back out, show the Suggestions row — Ready for a Showroom / Ready for a Collection. Do not create one. A collection is where an inventory dump becomes an identity — Crown Jewels is one from each game. The app also reads your inventory and proposes the groupings you haven't made yet.
2:45–3:20 Showroom Open Shrine of the Sovereign. Tap Enter the room. Drag to shift the parallax. Tap an item, then View in 3D — let the mesh rotate. And this is where a verified collection becomes a room you can walk into. These are real 3D meshes, baked from our own concept art — no publisher assets anywhere. Only verified items get a pedestal. That's the reward for proving what you own.
3:20–3:40 Explore Explore tab. Collectors — point at a match percentage and its reason. Switch to Communities. Because your inventory is now data, we can match you to collectors who own what you own — and always tell you why. Communities group people by what they collect rather than what they play.
3:40–3:52 News & assistant Home → a news card → open the reader. Then tap the assistant and ask one short question. Have the question typed and ready. Skin news, summarised and ranked for the games you follow. And an assistant that answers from your own collection — how many verified items you have, which sets you could finish.
3:52–4:00 Guest & close Cut to the guest session already open on Collections, showing the sign-up pitch. Pre-record this — don't sign out live. You can browse all of it as a guest and own none of it — which is the pitch. Collectee: every skin you own, in one place, finally worth showing.

Recording notes

  • Rehearse the import twice. It's 70 of your 240 seconds and the only segment with a live model call. Everything else is instant.
  • Use these two files — measured, not guessed. 4 Rare · Charm — Dog Tag (CODM, 0.92) and 2 Legendary · Sovereign Ghost (Valorant, 0.90) are the only two that clear the auto-accept threshold and add themselves.
  • Two files, not one. Multi-upload is a feature, and it gives you one auto-add plus one Confirm — which shows both halves of the trust model in a single pass.
  • Never demo these: AK117 — Cordite Storm, Mace — Blackout, Camo — Desert Strata, Glitchpop Vandal, Lancelot — Royal Matador. They import the wrong item.
  • Word budget. The voiceover is ~530 words ≈ 3:35 at a natural pace. That leaves ~25 seconds of slack for the scan to run — don't fill it with more script.
  • If the scan is slow, keep talking over it. The line about "never guesses on my behalf" is written to be said while the spinner runs.
  • Trim here first if you're over: cut News & assistant (12s), then Explore's Communities half (8s). Never cut Import or the Showroom.
  • Say "our own concept art" out loud during the room. It pre-empts the first question a publisher asks.

What each beat is proving

  • Import — the app does something real and hard, live, on stage. This is the differentiator.
  • Verified vs unverified — you thought about trust before anyone asked.
  • Showroom — the emotional payoff, and the reason verification is worth doing.
  • Match reasons — the social layer is explainable, not a black box.
  • Guest — you understand the funnel, not just the feature list.
