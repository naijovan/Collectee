COLLECTEE — ANIMATION BRIEF
===========================================================================
Garena AI Build Challenge · Singapore · every asset here exported from the
real build, not mocked up.


WHAT COLLECTEE IS, IN ONE PARAGRAPH
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every gamer owns hundreds of skins, and every one of them is locked inside
the game that sold it. Collectee turns them into one collection you own the
identity of, across Call of Duty: Mobile, Valorant and Mobile Legends. You
photograph your inventory, a vision model reads the screenshots and matches
each skin to a catalogue, and what comes out the other side is yours to group,
rank and show off. Verified items — ones backed by a linked game account —
earn a place in a 3D showroom other collectors can walk through.


THE ONE IDEA THE ANIMATION SHOULD CARRY
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Scattered -> gathered -> displayed.

    scattered   skins trapped in separate games, unreachable
    gathered    a scan pulls them into one place and names them
    displayed   the ones you can prove become a room worth visiting

If a single motion has to say what this product is, it is loose cards from
three different worlds converging into one stack, then that stack opening
into a lit room. The logo is literally that shape already: three cards, fanned.


TONE
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Dark, neon, confident. Not playful, not corporate.

    - Near-black backgrounds (#0B0D10). The art is the only bright thing.
    - Light comes from the objects themselves — rim glow, not key light.
    - Motion is springy and settles. Overshoot slightly, never bounce twice.
    - Nothing spins. Nothing flies in from off-screen for the sake of it.

The app's own spring is friction 9 / tension 90. Matching it will make the
animation feel like the same product rather than a trailer bolted on.

THE LOGO IS BUILT TO BE ANIMATED
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
01_BRAND/collectee-logo.svg has four named groups:

    #card-back   #card-mid   #card-front   #sparkle

Each card is split into a -fill (dark face) and a -rim (neon edge), so the
edges can charge up independently of the faces.

The cards are drawn ALREADY FANNED. To build the fan on screen, start all
three at #card-front's transform and stagger them out to their own — that
reads as a stack being dealt. Pop the sparkle after the cards settle, not
during.

It is a REDRAW, not a trace. The shipped mark is a raster render with a
volumetric glow that has no faithful vector equivalent. Use the SVG for
motion; use collectee-logo.png when the exact look matters.


THE MOMENT THAT MATTERS MOST
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Unverified -> verified.

03_ANIMATION_ASSETS/verified-badge.svg holds both states with the tick as its
own group. Scale the tick from zero with a slight overshoot while the pill's
border sweeps grey (#6B7484) to green (#31C48D).

That transition is the product's whole payoff: it is the instant an item
stops being a claim and earns a pedestal. If one beat gets extra attention,
this is it.


HARD CONSTRAINTS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. NO PUBLISHER ASSETS. Every image here is original prototype art. There are
   no game logos, no real character likenesses, no publisher marks anywhere in
   this pack, and none may be added. If the animation needs a game's mark it
   has to be cleared first — do not source one.

2. DO NOT RECOLOUR THE LOGO. The blue-to-violet is the brand gradient. A
   version tinted to a game's colours would imply a partnership that does not
   exist.

3. ONE GRADIENT ONLY. #3B82F6 -> #9333EA at 135 degrees. It is on every
   primary button, the active chip and the import progress rail. Do not
   introduce a second gradient; if something needs emphasis it takes this one
   or it takes a flat accent.

4. GAME COLOURS ARE NOT INTERCHANGEABLE. CODM is ember/amber, Valorant is
   red with a teal label, Mobile Legends is violet. Swapping them is a factual
   error about which game a skin belongs to. Full pairs in the brand guide.


EVERYTHING ELSE
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
01_BRAND/brand-guide.pdf — one page: colours with hex codes, the gradient,
per-game accents, the rarity scale, type, radius and spacing, shadows, the two
different hover treatments and when each applies, the scrim rule for text over
artwork, icon treatment and logo rules.

Every value in it is read from the app's source (src/theme/theme.ts), not
sampled from a screenshot. If the guide and the app ever disagree, the app is
right.
