Gradient — the one brand gradient

Used on every primary CTA, the active filter chip, the import step rail and the logo. Always 135° (top-left → bottom-right). Do not invent a second gradient; if something needs emphasis it takes this one or it takes a flat accent.

The two hovers are not interchangeable. A card in a wrapped grid that scales overlaps its neighbours and pushes its border outside the container; a chip in a spaced row has room, and a 3px lift on it is invisible.

Overlay treatment — how text sits on artwork

Cards do not put labels in a panel below the art. The art fills the tile and the text is laid over a bottom-weighted scrim, so the card spends its height once instead of twice.

  • Scrim: a real linear gradient, clear → medium → heavy at stops 0 / 0.45 / 1, covering the bottom 52–62%.
  • Corner marks: game badge top-left, owner or attribution top-right — the two frame the art between them.
  • Bottom band: title left, counts right. Likes are #F04438; item counts are white at 75%.
  • Never place white text on raw artwork. The scrim is what makes it legible on a bright crop.

Icon treatment

  • Tab icons are drawn line icons, 23–24px, not an icon font. Active takes the accent; inactive takes #6B7484.
  • The Import action is the exception: a filled gradient disc, raised, with a spring hover. It is the app's one primary action and looks like it.
  • Badges (rarity, trust, game) are pill-shaped, 1px border, transparent fill — the colour lives on the border and the label, never as a solid block over artwork.

Logo rules

  • Clear space — at least 20% of the mark's width on every side. It is drawn with its own margin; the files in animation/logo/ are trimmed, so add it back.
  • Minimum size — 24px. Below that the three cards merge and it reads as a blob; use a single card if you need smaller.
  • Backgrounds — near-black #0B0D10 is the intended home. On light backgrounds use collectee-mark-on-light.png; the glow disappears on white if you use the transparent version.
  • The glow is part of the mark. Do not flatten it away or add a second one.
  • Do not recolour it. The blue→violet is the brand gradient; a game-accent version would claim a partnership we do not have.
  • Do not rotate, skew or outline it.
  • Do not place it on artwork without a scrim behind it.
  • In the app it is a home button, 40px, sitting on the screen title's line — it is a lockup with the words, not a badge in the corner.
